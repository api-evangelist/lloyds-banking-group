# Lloyds Banking Group

> Lloyds Banking Group is one of the UK's CMA9 banks, publishing UK Open Banking (OBIE / PSD2) APIs through a public developer portal at developer.lloydsbanking.com across the Lloyds Bank, Halifax, and Bank of Scotland brands. It exposes unauthenticated Open Data reference APIs and the FAPI-secured Read/Write family (Account Information, Payment Initiation, Confirmation of Funds, Variable Recurring Payments, and Event Notifications), all conformant to the OBIE Read/Write API Standard.

## APIs

- [Open Data API](https://developer.lloydsbanking.com/prod01/lbg/opendata_lloyds): Public, unauthenticated reference data - ATMs, branches, personal/business current accounts, unsecured SME loans, commercial credit cards. Confirmed live at https://api.lloydsbank.com/open-banking/v2.2.
- [Account and Transaction Information (AIS)](https://developer.lloydsbanking.com/prod01/lbg/products): Consented read access to accounts, balances, transactions, beneficiaries, standing orders, direct debits, statements. FAPI-secured.
- [Payment Initiation (PIS)](https://developer.lloydsbanking.com/prod01/lbg/products): Domestic, scheduled, standing-order, international, and file/bulk payments with PSU authorisation.
- [Confirmation of Funds (CBPII)](https://developer.lloydsbanking.com/prod01/lbg/products): Check whether funds are available on a customer account.
- [Variable Recurring Payments (VRP)](https://developer.lloydsbanking.com/prod01/lbg/products): Consent-based recurring and sweeping payments under a customer mandate.
- [Event Notifications](https://developer.lloydsbanking.com/prod01/lbg/products): Real-time push (callback) and aggregated-polling event delivery to registered TPPs.
